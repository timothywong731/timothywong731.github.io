Beyond his professional work, Tim is a dedicated hobbyist woodworker with expertise in panel carving and CNC machining. In his personal workshop, he combines his technical expertise with a passion for artistry, creating intricate and customised designs for 3D wood panel carving. Tim brings his unique digital creations to life as one-of-a-kind commissioned pieces, using only high-quality materials, including a variety of native and exotic solid hardwoods. Discover a selection of his recent projects in the gallery below.
